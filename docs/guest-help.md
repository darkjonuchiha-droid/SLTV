# TV not showing?

1. Enable media: Preferences → Sound & Media → check "Media enabled"
   (Firestorm: also the filmstrip icon in the top bar).
2. Click the TV screen once — if you see a darkened screen with a big
   translucent ▶ play button, that click starts the video and sound.
3. Still black? Right-click the screen → "Reload" on the media controls,
   or step off and back onto the parcel.
Volume is YOUR viewer's media slider — it never affects other people.
